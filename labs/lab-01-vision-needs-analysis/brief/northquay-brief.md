# NorthQuay Retail Group — visual automation brief

**Client:** NorthQuay Retail Group (fictional, created for this course)
**Estate:** 41 stores in one market, plus one online catalogue
**Requested by:** six departments, independently, within the same quarter

## What has happened

Six departments have each asked the technology team for "AI images". None of the
six requests arrived with a volume figure, a turnaround requirement or a review
owner. Your job is to establish which of them actually need a vision system, and
to specify the pipeline for the ones that do.

## Hard constraints

These are not preferences. A proposal that breaches any of them is rejected
without further review.

1. **Cost ceiling — S$1.20 per delivered asset**, all-in, including review time.
   Anything above this is cheaper to keep doing by hand at current wage rates.
2. **Turnaround — 48 hours maximum** from request to delivered asset for
   catalogue work; 4 hours for social response work. Nothing in this programme
   is real-time.
3. **Customer-image privacy** — no image containing an identifiable customer may
   leave the store network, be used as training or conditioning input, or be
   stored outside the in-market environment. Colleague images require written
   consent held by HR and are still restricted to the in-market environment.

## Deliverables

1. A completed needs-analysis scoring matrix covering all six candidate cases.
2. A one-sentence rejection rationale for every case you do not take forward,
   naming the criterion and the number that caused it.
3. A five-stage pipeline specification for each case you do take forward:
   SENSE, REPRESENT, ANALYSE, DECIDE, ACT/SYNTHESISE — with a named measurement
   at ANALYSE and a numeric threshold at DECIDE.

## What "need" means here

A vision system is needed where the task is **visual**, **repeated at volume**,
**consistent enough to specify**, and **currently expensive**. A task that fails
any one of those is usually better served by a template, a stock library, or by
leaving it alone.
