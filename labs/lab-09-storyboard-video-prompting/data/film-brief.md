# NorthQuay 'Harbour Line' AW26 — 24-second film brief

**Reference:** NQ-HARBOUR-FILM-AW26
**Total duration:** 24 seconds
**Delivery:** 16:9 master; 1:1 and 9:16 renditions cut from it (see Lab 11)

## Mandatory beats

Every one of these must appear somewhere in the 24 seconds. A beat with no shot
is a brief failure, not an editorial choice.

1. **Place and hour** — the harbour at first light, established clearly enough
   that a viewer knows where and when without a caption.
2. **The product working** — the jacket doing something, not just being worn.
   The collar against the wind is the agreed action.
3. **Material honesty** — at least one shot close enough to read the shell
   texture and the seam.
4. **A held ending** — the film resolves rather than stopping.

## The 8-second constraint

This exercise chooses an 8-second base-shot constraint, not a universal model maximum. 24 seconds is therefore
**three shots**, not one clip, and the three must hold continuity between them
without a shared render.

## What must not drift between shots

The product, the wardrobe, the model, the time of day, the weather, the lighting
direction, the colour palette and the lens. These go in the continuity bible and
are copied byte-for-byte into every shot prompt.

## What must not appear

Rendered text or logos inside the frame (they go on in post, where they stay
editable and legible), additional people, lens flare, and any change to the
jacket shell colour.

## Review

The animatic is reviewed before any generation spend. The reviewer answers four
questions only: which beat is uncovered, which shot is too long, which continuity
field is least likely to hold, and what single change most improves the film.
