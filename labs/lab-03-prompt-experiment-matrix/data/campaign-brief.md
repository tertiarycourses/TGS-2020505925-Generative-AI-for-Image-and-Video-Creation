# NorthQuay 'Harbour Line' AW26 — image brief

**Campaign reference:** NQ-HARBOUR-LINE-AW26
**Product:** the Harbour Line jacket — slate-blue shell, single chest seam,
stand collar, matte finish, no visible branding on the garment itself.

## The complaint that started this

Marketing generated 40 hero candidates last month and rejected 34 of them
because "the AI keeps changing the product". No one recorded what changed, by
how much, or which prompt produced which output. This lab replaces that argument
with a measurement.

## What the brief actually constrains

| Factor | Constraint |
|---|---|
| SUBJECT | The jacket as described above, worn open over a plain cream tee by an adult model, three-quarters to camera |
| CONTEXT | Harbour boardwalk at first light; mooring bollards mid-ground; wet timber underfoot |
| STYLE | Documentary product photography; restrained colour; natural contrast; no lens flare |
| TECHNICAL | 35mm equivalent, shallow depth of field, low warm key from camera left, 4:5 delivery |

## What the brief does NOT constrain

Weather beyond "clear with low haze", the model's appearance beyond "adult", and
the exact bollard arrangement. Anything not listed above is free to vary, and
your experiment should show it varying.

## Acceptance

A prompt is accepted when four variants generated from it hold the subject and
the composition within the tolerances measured in this lab, and when the prompt
is versioned so a colleague can repeat the documented configuration; stochastic output pixels need not repeat.
